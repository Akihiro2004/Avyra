Avyra

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Avyra is an open-source Android music player. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: Avyra has no account system and no analytics, and the developer runs no server that collects your data.

Data kept on your phone

Your listening history and Replay statistics, your settings, your queue, search history, downloads and local playlists are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, Avyra asks Google for permission to use one hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

  • What is saved there: your listening history (by month), your Avyra settings, and the name you give each device you back up.
  • Where it goes: only to your own Google Drive, directly from your phone. The developer never receives it.
  • What it is used for: restoring your data on a new phone and combining listening from your devices into Replay.
  • How to remove it: in Avyra, disconnect Drive backup and delete its devices; or in Google Drive on the web, open Settings > Manage apps > Avyra > Delete hidden app data. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Avyra's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone.

Services you choose to connect

When you use these features, your phone talks to the service directly, under that service's own privacy policy:

  • YouTube Music for search, streaming, your library and, if you sign in, your account's playlists and history.
  • Spotify, if you import playlists or enable animated artwork.
  • Last.fm and ListenBrainz, if you connect them, receive the songs you play (scrobbling).
  • Discord, if you connect it, shows what you are playing on your profile.
  • Lyrics and artwork providers receive the title and artist of the song being looked up.
  • Music sources and addons that you add yourself receive the searches and requests needed to play from them.
  • Listen Together: if you join or start a party, the party server receives the display name you choose, a device identifier and the shared queue for as long as the party lasts. It stores nothing in a database.
  • Update check: the app asks GitHub for the latest Avyra release.

Sign-in details

Sign-in cookies and tokens for the services above are stored encrypted on your device and are only sent to the service they belong to. They are excluded from Google Drive backup.

Children

Avyra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
