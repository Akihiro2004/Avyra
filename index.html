Avyra

A music player for Android

Open source, built around artwork, playback controls and a clean library.

Download Source code

What it does

  • Search, browse and play music through YouTube Music.
  • Play songs saved on your phone, including lossless and hi-res files.
  • Synced lyrics, gapless playback, crossfade and AutoMix, and an equalizer.
  • Cast to Chromecast and Google speakers, and continue in Android Auto.
  • Replay statistics of what you listen to, kept on your phone.
  • Optional backup of your listening history and settings to your own Google Drive.
  • Optional Last.fm, ListenBrainz and Discord connections.

Before installing

Avyra is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, Spotify, Apple, Discord or any other service it can connect to. It does not host any music.
